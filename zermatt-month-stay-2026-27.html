Zermatt month-long ski stay, winter 2026-27

1 to 3 people, washing machine, under 10k for the month. Prices pulled 10 Sep 2026. Airbnb figures are the monthly total Airbnb quoted for 2 adults with its "Washer" filter on. USD unless marked CHF. Rate used: 1 CHF = 1.235 USD.

The budget works in Zermatt village for January, February and March, and does not work for a December stay that crosses Christmas. With the washer filter on, Zermatt village has one to five units per month in the $5,700 to $9,500 range. The cheapest washer unit in the village over Christmas and New Year was $16,600.

Täsch, the village 12 minutes down the shuttle train, cuts lodging roughly in half. Washer units there run $3,350 to $6,600 a month in every winter month, December included.

Lift pass and tourist tax add about $1,550 per person for the month. Whether 10k "includes skiing" decides which options fit, so both views are shown below.

Where the budget breaks

  • Christmas to New Year in the village. Any month window that includes 20 Dec to 3 Jan roughly doubles village rent. A December stay that ends before 20 Dec, or starts 3 Jan, was not tested and is the one December option worth checking.
  • Three people in the village. Two-bedroom washer units in Zermatt start around $9,500 to $9,700 a month. Lodging alone squeaks under 10k, and three lift passes push all-in to $14k.
  • The March window that crosses Easter. Easter 2027 is 28 March. The Mar 5 to Apr 5 prices above already include it and were only about 5% above February, so it is a small effect.
  • Agencies. Zermatt Ski Chalets, Zermatt Stays, Zermatt Apartment Rentals, Luxury Chalet Co and Paragon all do seasonal lets but publish no monthly prices. Luxury Chalet Co's one listed seasonal unit was CHF 4,000 to 7,000 a week, so that tier is out. Zermatt Stays says it is always 5% under Airbnb and two of its units have in-unit laundry (Walgrand, sleeps 6, and Whitney, sleeps 10), so it is worth one email.

Cheapest path that still lives in Zermatt

January, Haus Select, 2 people, about $8,800 all-in. If the host confirms the washer, this is the only village option that fits all-in with lift passes. Backup: the Matterhorn View Apartment ($9,217, washer and dryer confirmed) or Chalet Gädi ($8,780, paid washer, sleeps 3).

February, the studio 6 minutes above Matterhorn Paradise station, $7,734. Washer and dryer in the flat, walkable to the Klein Matterhorn lift. All-in lands about $770 over 10k, so this is the pick if 10k means lodging only.

If the month is December, stay in Täsch. $4,325 for a 1 bedroom with parking. The shuttle is 12 minutes and runs until 1 am.

Airbnb versus the other channels (added 10 Sep 2026)

Airbnb in Switzerland dropped its separate guest fee in October 2025. Hosts now pay 15.5% and the price you see is the price you pay, plus tourist tax where the host does not include it. So Airbnb is no longer expensive because of fees. It is expensive because the hosts who allow one-month winter stays price for peak demand. The cheaper channels are the ones where owners think in seasons, not nights.
