# ski-rentals-2027 — working rules

Winter 2026-27 ski research: refundable rentals, the resorts worth the trip, and the Zermatt month-stay report.
Public repo, published at https://samueljwebber.github.io/ski-rentals-2027/ .

## What the user actually wants

Steep terrain first, then a short walk to the lift, then a real town. **Zermatt is the benchmark** every other
resort is judged against. Lift speed and real lift infrastructure count; marketing claims do not.

## Hard lines

- **This repo is PUBLIC.** No personal details, no booking references, no payment information.
- Every price carries its currency and the date it was quoted. Airbnb quotes in USD by default — an
  unlabelled figure has already caused one published error.
- A claim that cannot be sourced is cut or marked unsourceable, not softened. Several resorts have already been
  corrected this way (Andermatt queues, Fernie's Polar Peak, St Anton).
- Headlines state findings or are noun phrases, never commands.
