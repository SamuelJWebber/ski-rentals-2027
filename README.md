Two reports, both researched 10 September 2026 for the weeks of 9 Jan, 16 Jan, 23 Jan, 6 Mar and 13 Mar 2027, for 1 to 3 people, under US$3,000 a week, refundable, with a washer and a gym.

## [steep-and-town-2027.html](steep-and-town-2027.html) — steep skiing with a real town attached

North America and Japan, ranked on a strict priority order: (1) extreme terrain, (2) walk to the lift, (3) a real town with a gym, a supermarket and life after skiing. 19 resorts assessed with honest terrain write-ups, named gyms and supermarkets, base-to-town distances and shuttle details, lift-ticket and Ikon/Epic status, and 60 priced options.

Answer: **Telluride in January** in North America, **Hakuba** in Japan. Whistler, Fernie and Red Mountain are the cheaper runners-up; Crested Butte is the March substitute for Telluride.
